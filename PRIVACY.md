# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 5, 2026_

Phrase Selects is a panel for Adobe Premiere. It runs entirely inside Premiere on your computer.

## What it reads

When you click Use Selected Clip or Search Transcript, Phrase Selects reads the transcript of the clip or sequence you selected in the open Premiere project (in the Project panel or on the timeline), or of the active sequence if nothing is selected. It uses the transcript only to find the words you searched for.

## What it changes

When you click Create Outputs, Phrase Selects adds a bin, subclips and a sequence to the open project. It does not change or delete your existing clips, sequences or transcripts.

## What it stores

The panel saves your default handle times (the Before and After values set with Set Default) in Premiere's local storage for the plugin, on your computer.

## What it sends

Nothing. Phrase Selects has no network access: its manifest requests no network permission, so Premiere blocks it from connecting to the internet. It does not collect analytics, usage data or personal information, and it does not read or write files outside the Premiere project.

Questions about this policy: open an issue at <https://github.com/cris-pen/phrase-selects/issues>.
